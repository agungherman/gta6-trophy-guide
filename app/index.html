    Road to the platinum

    Four stages, in the order to do them. About 200 hours all in, at a relaxed pace — the story is 60 to 80 of those. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so keep a manual save at the start and the end of each chapter (the game gives you 15 manual save slots plus one autosave).

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy can be missed, and only in one spot. Everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story, so once the meditation below is done you can relax and play at your own pace.

      Why nothing else is missable

      • Every story trophy unlocks automatically by finishing the story — none of them can be missed.
      • Every quest giver with a trophy (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) is available again after the story, even the ones who disappear for a while mid-story.
      • Timed hourglass missions do expire, but no trophy needs them, and there are far more than the 100 optional missions the 100% checklist asks for.
      • Cheats disable trophies and saving for that session — do not use them on a trophy run.
