One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude. After the mission Down to Earth, find Lucia at Jason’s house in the morning — she mentions meditating. Follow her to the balcony and accept. Do that meditation before you finish Chapter 1. Everything else can be earned after the story, in free roam.

Roadmap to the platinum

Three phases, in order. The story first, then 100% completion, then cleanup. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter — you get 15 save slots plus an autosave.

  1. Phase 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours

    Finish all five chapters with Jason and Lucia. Story trophies pop as you go — the chapter trophies, the final mission, and the named missions (Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child’s Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory). Do optional missions as they appear, scan unfamiliar vehicles with the WAiNK app, and study animals with binoculars whenever you meet a new species — 200 base-model vehicles and 100 animal species are on the 100% checklist later. And do the balcony meditation in Chapter 1 (see the warning above).

  2. Phase 2 — Reach 100% completion about 100 hours

    Open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work the checklist. You only need set portions of most activities — not everything in the game. The table below lists every requirement.

  3. Phase 3 — Clean up 10–20 hours

    Finish the stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone — eight strands in total for Strangers & Freaks), the remaining meditation spots, and any combat, driving, robbery or miscellaneous trophies still open. When the last one pops, Vice Got a Price! follows.
